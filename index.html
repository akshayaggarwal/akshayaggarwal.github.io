About

I'm a site reliability engineer who likes the hard part of production: the hour when a system is misbehaving and nobody knows why yet. I've spent most of my career on distributed storage (ZippyDB and RocksDB), AI infrastructure, and release pipelines, and lately on autonomous agents that can do some of that diagnosis themselves.

I think AI is a once-in-a-decade shift for how software gets operated, so I'm spending my evenings learning it properly, building with it, and writing about what I see. Outside of systems, I'm a published poet and I write songs.
